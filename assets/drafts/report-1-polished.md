Title: Comprehensive Analysis of a Critical Authorization Vulnerability Affecting the Invoice Management Ecosystem

Executive Summary:
During a thorough security assessment, a critical vulnerability was identified in the InvoiceFlow invoice management ecosystem. This vulnerability could potentially allow malicious actors to compromise the confidentiality, integrity and availability of sensitive data across the entire platform.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Navigate to the application.
2. Authenticate using valid credentials.
3. Locate the vulnerable endpoint /api/v2/invoices/export.
4. Manipulate the relevant parameter.
5. Observe the unauthorized data disclosure.

Impact:
This could lead to severe financial and reputational damage, regulatory fines and loss of customer trust. CVSS 9.8 (Critical).

Recommendation:
Implement robust access controls and follow industry best practices.
